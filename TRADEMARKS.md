# Fedorium name and logo policy

The code and artwork in this repository are free to use, change and share under
their licenses (see [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md)). The **Fedorium** name and the
Fedorium hat-in-hexagon logo are how people recognise the official project, so
their use as an identifier follows these simple rules.

## You can, without asking

- Talk about Fedorium, review it, write tutorials and make videos about it,
  using the name and logo.
- Build the ISO yourself from this repository for your own use, and share
  unmodified official builds.
- Use the logo on a page or link that points to the official project.

## Please don't

- Call your own distribution, fork or remix "Fedorium" or anything that could be
  mistaken for it, or use the Fedorium logo as its logo.
- Distribute a modified ISO under the Fedorium name.
- Suggest that your product is made, approved or supported by Fedorium when it
  isn't.

## Forking

Forks are welcome. Before publishing one, give it its own name, replace the
logos and wallpapers in `branding/files/` with your own, and change
`NAME`/`ID` in `branding/files/fedorium-os-release`.

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
